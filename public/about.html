How it started

Built in 2026.
Built for the long haul.

VIABUS started with a simple observation: intercity bus travel in North America had been allowed to become a last resort. Uncomfortable, unreliable, and overlooked.

We believed it didn't have to be that way. That a coach — properly designed, properly operated, properly tracked — could be the best way to travel between cities.

So we built the infrastructure to make it real. A vetted network of premium operators. Live GPS on every vehicle. Booking in seconds. And a commitment to treating every passenger like a guest.

Our values

What we
stand for.

Four principles that shape every decision we make — from route planning to crew training.

01
Precision

Operations run on discipline. Schedules are kept. Vehicles are maintained. Data is accurate. We don't guess.

02
Warmth

Every passenger is a guest. The crew are trained in hospitality, not just driving. The difference shows in the small moments.

03
Transparency

Fares are all-in. Tracking is live. Delays are communicated before you ask. No surprises, ever.

04
Safety

Every operator is audited. Every vehicle is inspected. Every driver is vetted. Safety is not a feature — it's the floor.

Sustainability

The road
forward.

Every VIABUS journey removes an average of 4.2 cars from the road. We're committed to transitioning 30% of our fleet to electric coaches by 2028, and partnering only with operators who meet our emissions standards.

  • 30% electric fleet target by 2028
  • Carbon offset included on every ticket
  • Emissions reporting on all routes
  • ISO 14001 environmental certification in progress
